**Why Growth Is Accelerating**

One of the core reasons behind this growth is the intensifying global requirement for food. PGRs by helping plants grow faster, optimize growth cycles, and boost tolerance to stress offer a powerful tool to meet these demands.

**Barriers and Constraints**

Still, not all is smooth sailing. The adoption of quality PGRs often involves significant upfront costs both for the products themselves and for the equipment or technology needed for their application. For many smallholder farmers particularly in resource-limited settings these costs can be prohibitive. 

Environmental concerns also weigh heavily. The widespread use of synthetic PGRs raises the specter of soil or water contamination, which may deter adoption, especially in regions with regulatory scrutiny or greater ecological awareness. 
These constraints help explain why adoption is uneven concentrated in regions and among farmers with both financial capacity and access to modern agriculture infrastructure even as total global demand grows.
